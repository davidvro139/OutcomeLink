Each reporting period has a **Close-out** tab that walks it from open to submitted. Anyone can read it; administrators (System and Institutional) can act on it.

## The steps, in order

1. **Set the outcomes deadline** — recommended, never blocking. The dashboards count down to it and at-risk alerts use it.
2. **Compute results** — calculates every rate from the current student data.
3. **Run validation** — checks the data and the results against the benchmarks. It needs computed results first.
4. **Resolve validation errors** — open **errors** must be fixed or explained; **warnings** are shown for review but never block.
5. **Review off-track programs** — every program that is off track for a metric needs an **improvement plan** for this period.
6. **Sign off** — an administrator confirms the results have been reviewed and can add a note.
7. **Finalize** — locks the period against recomputation and outcome edits.
8. **Mark as submitted** — record that the report has gone to the accreditor.

Each step shows whether it's done, what is holding it back, and its action in place.

## What can stop you finalizing

**Sign-off is always required** and cannot be overridden. It applies to the exact results that were reviewed: if results are recomputed, validation is run again, or student data changes afterwards, the sign-off is out of date and must be repeated.

Four things are **blockers**: results missing or out of date (student data changed since they were computed), validation not run since, open errors, and off-track programs with no improvement plan. If any remain, an administrator can still finalize by entering an **override reason** — for example that the accreditor approved an exception. The reason is recorded on the period and in its audit history and shown afterwards.

## After finalizing

A finalized or submitted period shows its record: who signed off, the note, and any override reason. To change anything, **Reopen** the period with a reason. Reopening clears the sign-off, so the period must be reviewed and signed off again.
