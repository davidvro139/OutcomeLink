Placement and licensure rates depend on knowing what happened to each graduate. This guide covers finding graduates who still need an outcome, recording it, and contacting people.

## Find who needs attention

The **Follow-Up Queue** lists graduates with no recorded outcome, or still "seeking" or unknown, with how long since anyone last tried to reach them. Rows past their follow-up date are highlighted. You can assign a student to a staff member from the queue (if your role allows), and select several students to assign them, or log the same follow-up for all of them at once.

Programs can have a **follow-up owner** (Program → follow-up owner), and new work is assigned to that person automatically each morning. Overdue follow-ups notify the assigned person.

## Record what you learned

Open a student and use their tabs:

- **Follow-ups** — log each attempt: when, by phone, email, text or in person, what happened, and the next follow-up date.
- **Employment** — add a job: employer, title, start date, whether it is full-time and related to the training. Supporting evidence (a pay stub, a letter) can be attached and marked verified.
- **Enrollments & Outcomes** — an enrollment's status, and for each reporting period an **outcome record** (employed, continuing education, military, unavailable, refused, seeking…). "Related to training" needs a short justification.
- **Licensure** — record each exam attempt and its result when the program requires a license.

A graduate with **no outcome record** counts as _seeking or unknown_ for placement — that is why the queue exists.

## Surveys

From a student's **Surveys** tab you can send a **graduate survey** or an **employer survey** (the employer must be on the student's employment record). Choose the **EMAIL** channel to email the link straight away; any other channel creates a link for you to pass on yourself. Responses come back to the student's record.

The **outreach campaign** (on a reporting period's Reports tab) does the same for everyone in that period who still has no resolved outcome, skipping anyone with a survey already pending, anyone flagged do-not-contact, and anyone with no email address. If some emails fail, run the campaign again — it re-sends only those.

## Do-not-contact

Switch **Do not contact** on for a student who has asked not to be contacted. They will not be emailed surveys, are skipped by campaigns, and you can't log a follow-up attempt against them. Every change to the flag is in the student's audit history.
