## Signing in

Your administrator invites you by email. Follow the link in the message, choose a password (at least 8 characters), and sign in with your email address and that password. If you forget it, ask an administrator to send a password reset — there is no self-service reset. If the invitation email didn't arrive, they can give you the link directly.

## Finding your way around

The left-hand menu is grouped by what you're doing:

- **Dashboard** and **My Programs** — where things stand right now.
- **Students & Outcomes** — programs, students, employers, and the two work queues: the **Follow-Up Queue** (graduates whose outcome isn't known yet) and the **Licensure Queue** (graduates waiting on an exam result).
- **Accreditation** — reporting periods, trends and the **Report Builder**.
- **Administration** — bulk import, users, job history and settings. You only see the entries your role can use.

The search box at the top finds students, employers, programs and contacts. The bell shows notifications, such as a scheduled report being ready or a program falling behind. The **Help** entry at the bottom of the menu is this guide.

## What your role can do

| Role                            | Can do                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **System Administrator**        | Everything, including programs, campuses and outgoing-email settings.                                                                            |
| **Institutional Administrator** | Everything except editing programs, campuses and email settings: users, settings, imports, reporting-period lifecycle, and all operational work. |
| **Program Administrator**       | Operational work and student/enrollment/import changes for their assigned programs and campuses only.                                            |
| **Career Services Staff**       | Operational work (follow-ups, employment, outcomes, surveys, reports) and employers, institution-wide.                                           |
| **Instructor / Staff**          | Operational work: follow-ups, employment, licensure results, outcomes, surveys, reports. Cannot add students or employers.                       |
| **Read-Only Auditor**           | Look at anything in their scope and run and export reports. Cannot change records.                                                               |

If a button you expect isn't there, your role doesn't allow it — see the Q&A "Why can't I see…?".

## A first week

1. Open **My Programs** and read the status of each program you look after.
2. Open the **Follow-Up Queue** and work through graduates with no recorded outcome — see [Following up with graduates](/help/follow-up).
3. When a reporting period is nearly due, use the period's **Close-out** tab — see [Closing a reporting period](/help/closeout).
