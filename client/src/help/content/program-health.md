## My Programs

**My Programs** shows each program you can access as a card: its overall status, and for each of Completion, Placement and Licensure a bar with a tick at the **benchmark** (the accreditor's required rate, or a negotiated one), the current rate, and a sentence saying what that means. A **Needs attention** list at the top puts the most urgent items first, each with a link to where you can act.

## The statuses

| Status                | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Meeting benchmark** | The rate is at or above the benchmark.                                                                                                                                         |
| **At risk**           | Below the benchmark, but still reachable — for example placement, where graduates who are still seeking could yet be placed. The card says how many more successes are needed. |
| **Off track**         | Below the benchmark and **not reachable** even if every remaining student succeeds. The card shows the best rate still possible.                                               |
| **No data**           | The metric doesn't apply this period (for example licensure in a program with no licensed graduates) — not a failing zero.                                                     |

These are calculated from the **last computed results**, not from live data. The page states how old they are; press **Recompute now** (Program Administrators and administrators) to refresh — it recomputes the period for the whole institution and can take several seconds, and it can't be repeated within five minutes.

At risk and off-track programs are also flagged to the program's people by notification: right away for off track, and at 30 and 7 days before the **outcomes deadline** for at risk.

## Benchmarks

The standard benchmarks come from the accreditation rule set. A **negotiated benchmark** — a rate the accreditor approved for one program and metric over a date range — replaces the standard one for that program, and the card says so. Only System Administrators enter them (on the program's page).

## Related screens

- **Readiness** (a period's tab) — the same programs in a table with open validation issues and a ready/not-ready flag.
- **Trends** — one program's rates across reporting periods.
- **CPL Dashboard** (a period's tab) — the numbers behind every rate; click any percentage to see the students counted, who was excluded, and _why_ each student counts or doesn't.
- **Improvement Plans** — record the cause and the plan for a program that is below a benchmark. An off-track program needs one before its period can be finalized without an override.

You can **Export to Excel** from My Programs; the file includes a "Report Info" sheet saying when the results were computed.
